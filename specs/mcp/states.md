# Feature: states

> Description: The two cells of a rule, the flags beside them, the rollups
>   over them, the structured payload every surface reads, and the status
>   table a person reads. One reader assembles specs and the evidence into the
>   cells of every rule; the table, the dashboard, the sign-off walk and the
>   drift report all render that one payload rather than parsing each other's
>   output.
> Scope: scripts/mcp/purlin/states.py, scripts/mcp/purlin/payload.py, scripts/mcp/purlin/status.py, scripts/mcp/purlin/facts.py, scripts/mcp/purlin/project.py, scripts/mcp/purlin/wording.py
> Stack: python/stdlib, json, hashlib, subprocess (list-only)
> Highest-Rule: 116
> Highest-Proof: 278

## Rules

- RULE-42: An evidence file whose own `source` field disagrees with the folder it sits in is left out of the reading, and one warning naming its path is added to the payload's warnings

- RULE-45: A section that is out of date decides nothing: where the newest section that speaks for the rule is current, an older out-of-date one beside it is left out of `platforms` and its failures are not read
- RULE-6: A proof carrying `@env` is proved only by a current section from that operating system: the passed cell reads `not run`, names the operating system's stored word under `missing_env` and gives the reason `<System>: no run yet`, the system as a person reads it, `Windows`, `macOS` or `Linux/Unix`, rather than taking a word of its own
- RULE-7: Where the newest section that speaks for the rule is not current the passed cell reads `out of date`, not met and not current, with one reason per part that differs, `spec changed since <sha7>`, `code changed since <sha7>` or `tests changed since <sha7>` where the sha is the section's commit, and raises the `out_of_date` flag; a person's own section, committed or not, goes out of date like any other, and the next run clears it
- RULE-8: A rule some of whose proofs no test backs reads `no test`, with no source, not current and not counting, and the reason `no test for <PROOF-N>`, naming every such proof joined by `, `; a proof that no marker ties to a test and no current section lists with a test has no test, and a `@manual` proof needs none
- RULE-9: The passed cell reads `failed` when a current section observed a failing proof of the rule, reading a `@env` proof only from that operating system's section; its reasons name where, as `failing: <System>, <source>`, the system as a person reads it, and the `failing` flag is raised
- RULE-11: The strong cell of a rule whose passed cell is not met reads `waiting`, with the reason `waiting for its tests to pass`; `waiting` is never met and is not `weak`
- RULE-14: The strong cell reads the audit entry for the rule's current rule, proof and test hashes, its `verdict` and its `findings`, and no other field: a field the format does not name decides nothing, the cell carries the entry's findings under `findings`, an entry reading `weak` makes it read `weak` with each finding among its reasons, and the cell names the evidence file under `evidence`
- RULE-15: A rule whose tests pass and that no audit entry answers for its current rule, proof and test hashes reads `not audited` in its strong cell with the reason `no audit has run on this code`, or `the AI audit could not run: <why>` where the last audit could not reach the model for those hashes
- RULE-16: A rule with a `@manual` proof reads `checked at sign-off` in its strong cell, unless its audit reads `weak`, and raises the `manual` flag
- RULE-23: A rule's bucket is exactly one of `untested`, `failing`, `partial` and `passed`: `failing` where its passed cell reads `failed`, `partial` where it reads `partial`, `passed` where it reads `passed`, and `untested` where it reads any other word, a rule no proof line names included
