# Lane F15: the review list says something per row

Design: dev/plans/evidence-workflow.md (A3 `purlin:review`, A6 QA, B8 "Review list"); design/readme.md "Content fundamentals"
Rules: dev/plans/lanes/_rules.md (read in full); the phase 9 rules in dev/plans/lanes/9B.md (rule tags, the author flag)

The user looked at the Review list on this repository's board: under the group "High risk
(211)" every row reads `approvals RULE-3 [high] risk high needs a look`, and so on for 211
rows. The tag repeats the group; the reason repeats the tag. The row has to tell a QA
person what they are about to read and why this rule, not what every row shares.

## Change

- `scripts/report/src/review.js`: a row shows the feature, the rule id, the rule's text
  (from `DATA.features[].rules[]`, cut at about 90 characters with `…`), its state pill (the
  same pill the Board uses), and a reason only when it says more than the group does:
  `stale` (an approval went stale), `strength 48% under 80%`, a free-check finding by its
  plain sentence, `re-verify pending`, `windows: no record yet`. A rule whose only reason
  is its risk shows no reason. Drop the per-row risk tag inside a risk group. Keep the
  group header, the count, the order, the click-through.
- `scripts/mcp/purlin/payload.py` (and `states.py` if that is where it is built): the
  review list entry gets `reasons` as a list of short codes and texts rather than the one
  sentence `risk high needs a look`, keeping `reason` for one release as the joined text
  so nothing that reads it breaks (`brief.py`, the skills). Read `scripts/review/brief.py`
  and `skills/review/SKILL.md` for what consumes `reason` and keep them true.
- `dev/test_purlin_report.py`: adjust the review-list tests; add one that a high-risk rule
  with nothing else wrong shows no reason text and one that a stale rule shows `stale`.
  `dev/test_mcp_server.py`: one test for the `reasons` list. Proof markers on the
  affected rules of `specs/dashboard/purlin_report.md` and `specs/mcp/states.md` (edit the
  rule text where it states the old shape, keep numbers; new rules at the next free
  numbers, `[risk: low] [origin: eng]` for the page, `[risk: medium] [origin: eng]` for the
  payload).
- Rebuild the page, regenerate `docs/images/dashboard-review-list.png`, refresh the root copy.
