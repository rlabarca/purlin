Use `Logo` anywhere the brand signs the page — deck covers, the product top bar, document headers.

```jsx
<Logo size={44} />
<Logo variant="mark" size={64} />
```

The mark is a roof truss with copper measure lines. Two colourways only: cream webs for navy grounds (`assets/logo.svg`, the default) and navy webs with a darkened copper for paper and white (`assets/logo-light.svg`, `tone="light"`). Never redraw it, recolour it beyond those two pairs, or rotate it.

```jsx
<Logo tone="light" size={44} />
```
