Use `SectionLabel` for the small tracked caps that sit above a slide title, a panel, or a table group — "WAYS OF WORKING — 01", "A COVERAGE BOARD", "SPECS".

```jsx
<SectionLabel>Ways of working — 01</SectionLabel>
<SectionLabel tone="secondary" size="lg">A coverage board</SectionLabel>
```

Copper is the deck's eyebrow colour; blush is used when the eyebrow labels a panel rather than a section. Never bold it — tracking carries the emphasis.
